Privacy

Privacy & cookies.

Last updated Nov 10, 2026

In short: this website collects as little as possible. There are no advertising trackers, and fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties. If you send a message, I use your details only to reply to you. This site doesn’t use cookies or third-party analytics. You can ask me to see or delete your data at any time by emailing rnamocotdev@gmail.com.

Who I am

This website, rnamocotdev.com, is run by Renie Namocot, a freelance web developer based in the Philippines. I’m responsible for the personal data collected through this website (the “data controller”). You can contact me about privacy at rnamocotdev@gmail.com.

What I collect and why

When you send a message

If you use the contact form or the chat box, I receive your name, email address, the service and budget you selected, the page you sent it from, and your message. I use this only to reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to manage the project.

  • Messages are delivered to my email inbox (Gmail, provided by Google).
  • A backup copy is stored in a private, non-public folder on this website’s server, in case an email fails to arrive.
  • To stop spam, the form keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address for one hour to limit how many messages can be sent. Your actual IP address is not stored.

Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and my legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

Visitor counter

The visitor count in the footer is calculated on this website’s own server. It uses a scrambled code made from your IP address, browser type and today’s date to count each visitor once per day. That code can’t be turned back into your IP address, and the list is deleted every day. It doesn’t use cookies. Your browser remembers that you’ve been counted in “session storage”, which is cleared when you close the tab.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in knowing roughly how many people visit the site.

Server logs

Like almost every website, my hosting provider automatically records technical information when pages are requested, such as IP address, date and time, the page requested and browser type. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running, and are kept for a limited time by the host.

Analytics

This website doesn’t use Google Analytics or any other third-party analytics or advertising trackers. The only statistic collected is the visitor counter described above.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website doesn’t use cookies. That’s why you don’t see a cookie banner. The visitor counter uses your browser’s session storage, which never leaves your device and is cleared when you close the tab. If analytics is added in the future, it will only run after you agree to it, and this policy will be updated.

Who I share data with

I don’t sell or rent your personal data, and I don’t share it for advertising. It’s only handled by the services needed to run this website:

  • My web hosting provider, which stores the website, server logs and message backups.
  • Google (Gmail), which delivers and stores email.

These providers may store data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, they use safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long I keep data

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to a project: deleted within 2 years.
  • Client communications and project records: kept for as long as needed for the project and for legal, tax and accounting requirements.
  • Spam-limit data: 1 hour. Visitor counter data: 1 day.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act, the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and US state privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data I hold about you;
  • ask me to correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict how I use it;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where I rely on consent;
  • complain to your local data protection authority (for example, the National Privacy Commission in the Philippines, the ICO in the UK or the OAIC in Australia).

To use any of these rights, email rnamocotdev@gmail.com. I’ll reply within 30 days. I don’t sell personal data, so there’s nothing to opt out of under “do not sell” laws.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, the message backups and counter data are stored in a folder that can’t be reached from the web, and IP addresses are hashed rather than stored. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Children

This website is for businesses and isn’t aimed at children under 16. I don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle personal data, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
