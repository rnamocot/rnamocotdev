FAQ

Common questions.

Have another question? Email me.

Can you build a custom Shopify theme from scratch?

Yes. I build Online Store 2.0 themes from scratch in Liquid, based on your Figma design or brand guidelines.

Do you work with the Shopify REST and GraphQL APIs?

Yes. I build integrations with the Admin API (REST and GraphQL), the Storefront API and webhooks, including custom apps for syncing data with external systems.

What is Shopify Hydrogen?

Hydrogen is Shopify's React-based framework for building headless storefronts. It's a good fit when you need a fully custom front-end experience.

Can you migrate my WooCommerce store to Shopify?

Yes. I migrate products, customers and order history, rebuild the theme, and map 301 redirects to protect your search rankings.
