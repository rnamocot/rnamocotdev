FAQ

Common questions.

Have another question? Email me.

Do you work with Divi 5?

Yes. I work with current Divi releases and can help plan an upgrade or migration from older Divi versions.

Can you redesign my existing Divi site?

Yes. I can refresh the design, clean up legacy layouts and improve speed while keeping your content and SEO intact.

Can you migrate my site from Divi to Elementor or a custom theme?

Yes. I plan migrations page by page so URLs, metadata and rankings are preserved.

Is Divi good for SEO?

Divi sites can rank well when they are built cleanly, with a proper heading structure, fast loading and an SEO plugin configured correctly.
